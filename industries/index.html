Sustainability, energy, technology, operational excellence and agriculture consulting for the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Deep in the sectors that power the Kingdom.

Every sector has its own energy profile, regulators and pressure points. We focus where energy, emissions and compliance have the biggest impact on cost and reputation, and we speak the language of each sector.

Industrial & Manufacturing

The challenge

Energy is often a factory's second-largest operating cost. Major buyers now send ESG questionnaires with every tender, and certification is the entry ticket to new contracts.

How we help
  • Energy audits
  • Integrated ISO systems
  • GHG inventory
  • Supplier ESG readiness
  • Predictive maintenance

Real Estate & Facilities

The challenge

Cooling drives most of a building's energy use. Building codes and green certifications keep tightening, and tenants increasingly expect efficient, certified space.

How we help
  • Cooling optimisation
  • BMS tuning
  • Mostadam and LEED
  • Rooftop solar
  • SBC 601/602 compliance

Logistics & Warehousing

The challenge

Vast roofs, temperature-controlled space and large fleets, with clients asking more and more about the emissions in their supply chain.

How we help
  • Rooftop solar
  • Warehouse energy audits
  • Fleet emissions
  • Scope 3 reporting
  • ISO 50001

Hospitality, Tourism & Events

The challenge

Guests and event partners expect visible sustainability, and the Kingdom has a decade of world-class tourism and events ahead.

How we help
  • Hotel energy and water efficiency
  • Event carbon footprints
  • Sustainable event management (ISO 20121)
  • ESG reporting

Data Centres & Technology

The challenge

Power and cooling demand at a scale few sectors match, in one of the hottest climates on earth, with customers asking for low-carbon capacity.

How we help
  • Cooling efficiency
  • PUE improvement
  • Energy monitoring
  • Renewable sourcing feasibility
  • Carbon reporting

Mining & Utilities

The challenge

Water, emissions and community impact under close investor scrutiny, in one of Vision 2030's priority sectors.

How we help
  • GHG inventory
  • ESG reporting
  • Climate risk assessment
  • ISO 14001 and 45001

Government & Public Sector

The challenge

National targets, local content requirements and programmes that must show measurable results to leadership and the public.

How we help
  • Energy transition advisory
  • Public building efficiency
  • IKTVA and local content
  • Project sustainability management

Agriculture & Food Production

The challenge

Water scarcity, rising input costs and national food-security targets, with buyers and investors asking for proof of good practice.

How we help
  • Smart irrigation
  • Solar pumping
  • Fertigation
  • Precision agriculture
  • Saudi GAP readiness
