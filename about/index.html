Why we exist

Saudi organisations are being asked to do more than ever: cut energy use, disclose emissions, certify their systems and show progress on Vision 2030. Too often, that work is split between separate advisers who never compare notes.

We built Seenteco to bring it together. One team of doctors, engineers and certified specialists who understand how energy savings become carbon reductions, how carbon data becomes an ESG report, and how a management system keeps the results coming.

We are Saudi-based and Saudi-focused. We work to the Kingdom’s regulations, codes and programmes from day one, and we stay with our clients through implementation, not just until the report is delivered.
