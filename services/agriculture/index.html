Sustainability, energy, technology, operational excellence and agriculture consulting for the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

GROW

Grow more with less water.

Agriculture uses most of the Kingdom's water, and food security is a national priority. We help farms and agribusinesses cut water, fertiliser and energy costs with smart irrigation, solar power and precise nutrient management.

Who it is for. Commercial farms, greenhouse and hydroponic operators, date palm growers, agribusiness investors and food producers.

Smart irrigation & water efficiency (Drip · Sensors)

We design irrigation that delivers the right water, at the right time, to the right root zone.

Included
  • Water audit and irrigation survey
  • Drip and subsurface system design
  • Soil-moisture sensors and scheduling
  • Pump and network efficiency
You receiveAn irrigation upgrade plan with water savings, cost and payback.

Solar-powered farms (PV pumping)

Diesel pumps are costly and polluting. We size solar pumping and farm solar systems that pay for themselves.

Included
  • Energy and pumping load review
  • Solar pumping and battery sizing
  • Financial model and diesel comparison
  • Supplier specifications
You receiveA bankable feasibility study ready for suppliers and finance.

Soil & fertiliser management (Fertigation)

We match nutrients to what the soil and crop actually need, so less fertiliser goes to waste.

Included
  • Soil and water testing
  • Nutrient management plan
  • Fertigation design and scheduling
  • Soil health monitoring
You receiveA seasonal fertiliser programme with expected savings.

Precision agriculture & farm monitoring (IoT · Satellite)

See every field on one screen and act before problems spread.

  • Alert rules for your team

Greenhouses & protected agriculture

Cooling is a major cost in Saudi greenhouses. We cut it, and we test new growing models before you invest.

Included
  • Greenhouse energy and cooling audit

  • Technology and supplier review
