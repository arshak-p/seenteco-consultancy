ENERGY

Cut the costs. Keep the comfort. Prove the savings.

Energy is one of the largest controllable costs in Saudi buildings and factories, and cooling drives most of it. We find where energy is wasted, rank every fix by payback and verify the savings once they are in place.

Explore this practice→

Expertise.
Put to work.

Who it is for. Factories, commercial and residential buildings, warehouses, hotels, hospitals and universities.

Energy audits (ASHRAE L1–L3)

We measure where every kilowatt-hour goes, from chillers and lighting to compressed air and process loads, and turn it into a ranked list of savings.

Included
  • Site survey and spot metering
  • 12–24 months of bill analysis
  • Equipment and control checks
  • Costed savings measures
You receiveAn audit report and savings register with cost, savings and payback for each measure.

HVAC, chiller & cooling optimisation

Cooling is usually the largest load in a Saudi building. We tune it to use less energy without making anyone less comfortable.

Included
  • Chiller plant performance tests
  • Pump and fan review
  • Setpoint and schedule optimisation
  • Free-cooling and heat-recovery options
You receiveAn optimisation plan with quick wins and capital measures, and support to put them in place.

Lighting & motor efficiency

Proven, low-risk upgrades that often pay back fastest.

Included
  • Lighting and controls survey
  • Motor and drive inventory
  • Variable speed drive opportunities
  • Specifications for procurement
You receiveAn upgrade package with savings, payback and technical specifications ready for tender.

Rooftop solar & battery storage feasibility

We tell you whether solar makes sense on your site, how big it should be and what it returns.

Included
  • Roof and structural screening
  • Load profile analysis
  • System sizing and yield estimate
  • Financial model
  • Connection and permitting pathway
You receiveA bankable feasibility study you can take to suppliers and finance.

Energy efficiency compliance (SEEC · SBC 601/602)

We check that your buildings and equipment meet Saudi energy codes and standards, and fix what doesn't.

Included
  • Code compliance review for new or existing buildings
  • Envelope, HVAC and lighting checks
  • Equipment efficiency checks
  • Corrective action plan
You receiveA compliance report and the documents you need for approvals.

Energy management systems (ISO 50001)

Savings fade without a system to sustain them. We build one that keeps energy performance improving every year.

Included
  • Energy review and baseline
  • Energy performance indicators
  • Policies, procedures and roles
  • Internal audit and management review
You receiveA working ISO 50001 system, a trained energy team and readiness for certification audit.

Measurement & verification (IPMVP)

Independent proof that savings are real, to the protocol banks, energy service companies and auditors recognise.

Included
  • M&V plan and option selection
  • Baseline model adjusted for weather and occupancy
  • Metering plan
  • Periodic savings reports
You receiveVerified savings reports you can show your board, your lender or your auditor.
