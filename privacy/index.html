Privacy Policy

How Seenteco collects, uses and protects your information.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry or subscribe to our newsletter, we collect the details you give us: your name, company, job title, email address, phone number and message.

How we use it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry, prepare proposals and, if you subscribe, send our monthly updates. We do not sell your personal data.

Sharing

We share personal data only with service providers who help us run the website and email, and where the law requires it.

Keeping it safe

We keep your information only as long as needed for these purposes and take reasonable steps to protect it, in line with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or unsubscribe at any time, by contacting us through the contact page.
